The meaning behind our pillars

Six pillars, one living system. Each one answers a question every woman and community deserves answered.

01

Holistic Growth

Whole-person growth

Growth that sees the whole person and the whole community. Wellbeing, learning, livelihood and purpose move forward together, so change lasts for generations.

02

Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Ideas that become income

Backing women and communities as they turn ideas into ventures. We open pathways to build, test and scale businesses that create wealth for families and neighborhoods.

03

Community Building

Strength in belonging

No one rises alone. We build networks, spaces and shared responsibility so people are held up by each other.

04

Ethical Employment

Safety in standards

There is safety in standards, so we hold every workplace to them; true community begins with them.

05

Strategic Development

Vision with a plan

Careful planning, partnerships and operations that turn mission into measurable, sustainable results, in both the United States and Kenya.

06

Dignified Solutions

Respect in every answer

Every program and service is designed to preserve dignity and agency. We offer solutions with women and the community, not to them.
